A little about us

Small messages, made personal.

Kattygrapher is a small creative corner for making cat-themed digital greeting cards. It was made for the everyday moments when you want to send someone a smile, say what is in your heart, or make an ordinary day feel a little more special.

Why Kattygrapher?

Sometimes a short message says a lot. A birthday wish, an inside joke, a thank-you, or a simple “thinking of you” can mean more when it feels personal. Kattygrapher brings playful designs and your own words together so the greeting feels like yours.

Made for your kind of message

Choose a design, write a message in your own words, and create a card to share with someone you care about. Keep it sweet, silly, romantic, or completely your own.

The best part of a greeting card is the thought behind it. Kattygrapher is here to help you give that thought a cute little home.
