What is Cat Card Creator?

Cat Card Creator is a browser-based tool for designing personalized greeting cards. You can pick from more than a hundred wide and tall card templates, from soft watercolor florals to playful retro labels, then add your own words in a choice of lettering styles and colors. A friendly cat holds up each step, and your finished card appears on its sign, ready to download as a high-resolution image.

Why we built a free greeting card maker

A handwritten card still means something, but not everyone has the time, supplies or a shop nearby when a birthday, thank-you or “thinking of you” moment arrives. We wanted an easy, free way to make a thoughtful digital or printable card in minutes, with no design skills, no watermark and no account to create, and with a cat holding the sign, because everything is better with a cat.

How to make a custom greeting card online in 5 steps

  1. Choose a card design. Browse wide and tall templates and tap the one that fits the moment.
  2. Write your message. Type your own words, then try different fonts and text colors until it feels like you. Need inspiration? See our card message ideas.
  3. Add a memorable photo (optional). Upload a picture and it appears on your card like a little snapshot.
  4. Generate your card. Our cat gets to work and your finished card appears on the board.
  5. Download or make another. Save your card as an image to message, email or print, or start a new one for someone else.

Frequently asked questions about Cat Card Creator

Is Cat Card Creator really free?

Yes. The online greeting card maker is free to use, with no account, subscription or watermark. The site may show advertising to help cover running costs, as explained in our Privacy Policy.

How do I make a birthday card online for free?

Open the card maker, choose a birthday-friendly design, type your message, pick a lettering style and color, optionally add a photo, and download your finished card as an image. It takes about a minute.

Do you keep my photos or messages?

No. Your text and photo are processed in your own browser and are not uploaded to our servers. When you refresh or leave the page, they are gone.

Can I print the cards I make?

Yes. Cards download as a high-resolution PNG image. Print on regular paper, photo paper or light card stock, then fold and add a handwritten note.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Cat Card Creator works in modern browsers such as Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge on phones, tablets and computers. There is nothing to install.

What occasions can I make cards for?

Birthdays, thank-you notes, friendship, love and anniversaries, congratulations, get-well wishes, apologies and everyday just-because cards. Our Card Ideas page has message examples for each.

Can I use my cards commercially?

Cards are for personal, non-commercial use. See our Terms & Disclaimer for details, or contact us if you have a special request.

Something is not working. What should I do?

Refresh the page or update your browser. If the problem continues, send us your device, browser and a short description on the Contact page and we will look into it.
