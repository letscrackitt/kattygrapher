Cat Greeting Card Message Ideas

Looking for the right words for a cat-themed greeting card? Use these short ideas as inspiration, then personalize your digital card with a message that sounds like you.

Birthday messages

  • Hope your birthday is full of naps, treats and happy surprises!
  • Another year older, another year cattier. Happy birthday!
  • Wishing you a purr-fect birthday and a wonderful year ahead.

Thank-you messages

  • Thanks a meow-llion for being there for me.
  • You made my day brighter—thank you so much!
  • Sending a little cat love to say a big thank you.

Just-because messages

  • Just a reminder that you're pawsome.
  • Sending you a tiny card and a whole lot of love.
  • Hope this brings a smile to your face today.

Get-well and encouragement messages

  • Take it one cozy step at a time. I'm rooting for you.
  • Sending warm wishes, gentle purrs and brighter days ahead.
  • You don't have to do it all today. Rest up and be kind to yourself.

Tips for a personal card

  • Use the recipient's name or an affectionate nickname.
  • Mention a shared memory or an inside joke.
  • Keep the message natural; a short sincere note can mean more than a long one.
  • Preview your design and check spelling before sharing it.
