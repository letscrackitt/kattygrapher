How to create your digital card

  1. Open the creator. Visit the Kattygrapher card maker.
  2. Choose a cat-themed design. Pick a look that suits your occasion and recipient.
  3. Write your own message. A nickname, shared memory, or inside joke can make it feel special.
  4. Preview and download. Check the text and layout, then download your finished card using the creator's available options.

A digital greeting is most meaningful when it sounds like you. Use message suggestions for inspiration, then personalize the wording so it reflects your relationship.

Tips for a greeting that feels thoughtful

  • Be specific: mention one thing you appreciate about the person.
  • Match the tone to the occasion—funny, affectionate, gentle, or celebratory.
  • Keep the message readable and avoid filling the card with too much text.
  • Preview the final design before sharing it.
